Why GPSMap Needs Traccar?
We use it as a communication platform for your GPS devices.
Traccar how it works?
The process is very simple:
GPS Device → Traccar → GPSMap → Odoo

Your GPS device obtains vehicle information.
The GPS sends the data to Traccar.
Traccar receives and processes the information.
GPSMap connects to Traccar.
GPSMap stores the data in Odoo.
